Who We Are

About Ambika High School

Nurturing young minds since our establishment, we are committed to excellence in education and holistic development.

A Legacy of Quality Education

Ambika High School Tonki has been a beacon of quality education in Tonki, Teh. Manawar, Dist. Dhar, M.P.. Founded with the vision of providing accessible and excellent education to the children of our community, we have grown into a trusted institution that parents and students rely on.

Our school offers a nurturing environment where students from Nursery to Class 10 develop academically, socially, and personally. With dedicated teachers and modern facilities, we ensure every child receives the attention and guidance they deserve.

We believe in the power of education to transform lives. Our curriculum blends rigorous academics with co-curricular activities, preparing students not just for examinations, but for life.
